How a jar is made

From the north slope to your shelf, and back.

  1. 1

    Gather

    Moss and ferns are collected from the farm’s shaded north slope, a few handfuls at a time, always leaving the colony.

  2. 2

    Ferment

    Nine weeks in glass at a steady 18 degrees, tasted, smelled and logged by hand every Friday.

  3. 3

    Blend

    Small batches of sixty jars. Each one is labelled with the date it was poured and the tank it came from.

  4. 4

    Grow back

    Return the jar, plant the packaging, repeat. The loop is the product.
