About

Slow hands, short deadlines kept.

I draw at a table by a north-facing window, mostly in the morning, mostly with a dip pen. A job starts with a week of small ugly sketches, moves to a clean pencil layout you approve, and only then gets inked and washed. You see every stage, and nothing is a surprise.I draw at a table by a north-facing window, mostly in the morning, mostly with a dip pen. On the table

  • Walnut ink and a steel nib

How a job runs

  1. A call and a brief
  2. Thumbnails in a week
  3. Pencil layout, one round of notes
  4. Final ink and wash, scanned at 1200 dpi
