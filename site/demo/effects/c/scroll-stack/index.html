1. 01

    Gather

    Step 1

    Collect what you have: notes, files and half-ideas, in one place.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Step 2

    Cut it down to what matters and give it a clear order.

  3. 03

    Build

    Step 3

    Make the smallest version that works, then use it for real.

  4. 04

    Review

    Step 4

    Read it back with fresh eyes and fix what snags.

  5. 05

    Ship

    Step 5

    Release it quietly, watch what happens, and keep improving.
