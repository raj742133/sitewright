Dashboards & pages

12 whole screens, ready to import

Dashboards, landing and 404 pages and a brand board. They are chosen with effects.templates or --templates, not with --components. Live previews are coming to this page.

Brand 1

  • Bento board

    A share-ready brand board in a bento grid: statement, key number, logo, type sample, colour swatches, an abstract picture drawn from the brand colours, tone words, a slice of UI and a quote; reflows from four columns to one with its container.

    Original

    --templates bento-board
